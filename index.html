WHAT'S NEW

Update 2.2

  • New player modelsEvery player has their own look: hair, headbands, hats, masks, sleeves, shoes and build, in every game and in the shop.
  • New shopEvery card shows the player's portrait. Tap a player to open their profile, with an animated scene of them in action, real ratings, a backstory, their play style and fun facts. Buy or equip right from the profile.
  • SubstitutionsBring up to five bench players into a tournament. Players tire as they play. Pause and choose Substitutes, or let auto subs bring in fresh legs.
  • Legend tournamentsTake on the hardest opponents for 50% bigger coin and XP bonuses.
  • Longer gamesQuick play and tournaments can now be 8 or 10 minutes long.
  • New shootingThe meter shoots up to the very top, speeding up, then falls back down, slowing down. The small green at the very top always goes in, on the way up or down. Blaze and Snipe get a 25% bigger green in their zones, and Adapt gets 20% bigger at 100% and 30% at 135%.
  • StealsPress K (or the steal button) to reach when the dribbler is exposed. Reach during a crossover and you go down. Mashing drains your stamina.
  • Pressure defenseHold K (or the steal button) to shadow the ball instead of your man: you jump passing lanes and scoop up loose balls.
  • Block guideWhen someone shoots near you, a ring shows where you need to be, and in the air you see whether your hands are high enough.
  • Rookie and Pro tournamentsPractice tournaments for fewer coins. Only All-Star and Legend wins count as titles.

FRIENDS

Stay safe: only add people you know. Never share your real name, address, school, phone number, social media or passwords. Use Block or Report if someone makes you uncomfortable.

MESSAGES

Safety: messages are filtered, and links, emails, phone numbers, other apps and personal questions are blocked. Never meet up with someone you only know online.
