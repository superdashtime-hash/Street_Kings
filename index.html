How to earn XP

Finishing a game gives 20 XP, and winning gives 40 more. You also earn XP for points, steals, blocks and rebounds. In a tournament, each round you win gives 50 XP and winning the title gives 300.

WHAT'S NEW

Update 2.1

  • Player profilesTap a name on the leaderboards, friends list or hangout (or your own name) to see their level, quick match wins and online status, and send a friend request.
  • Tournaments saveLeave a tournament at any time and resume it later from the same round, even on another device with a cloud account.
  • Friends and messagesAdd friends, see who is online, challenge them in one click and send filtered messages. Block, Report and privacy settings are in Settings > Social.
  • Hangout quick chatPress 1-8 in the hangout to send a quick chat bubble.
  • Online statusLeaderboards now show who is online.
  • New animationsNew passing, shooting, stealing, blocking, crossover and celebration animations.
  • Fair steals and blocksNo more dice rolls. Reach the ball side of the dribbler to steal, jump on time to block, and stand in the lane to pick off passes. Rings show when you can steal.
  • Apex reworkHis new Afterimage lets him blink back to a marked spot and leave defenders behind.
  • Free coinsUse code NEWUI for 1,000 coins.

FRIENDS

Stay safe: only add people you know. Never share your real name, address, school, phone number, social media or passwords. Use Block or Report if someone makes you uncomfortable.

MESSAGES

Safety: messages are filtered, and links, emails, phone numbers, other apps and personal questions are blocked. Never meet up with someone you only know online.
